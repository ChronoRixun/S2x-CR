# Screenshot catalogue — 103 in-game captures

All files live in `D:\S2x-showcase\showcase\shots\`. Reference them from a page in
`candidates/` as `../shots/<filename>`.

Every one is a genuine unedited capture from S2x-CR. Index numbers match
`shotlist.txt` line numbers and the yellow labels on the contact sheets.

`W×H` is the real pixel size — put it on the `<img>` as width/height. Sizes vary
because some were captured windowed; don't assume 16:9.

## Notes on use

- **102 is the single most valuable image on this list.** It is independent evidence that
  other people host servers on this fork. It also reads 0/18 on every row — see the
  honesty rule in the brief.
- **005 and 018 together** are the bot-fill argument: a full 9v9 War lobby, and a
  Domination match where the bots actually put 114 points on the board.
- **030** is the most alive-looking scoreboard in the set (26–25 with nine minutes left).
- **052, 078, 081** carry the Zombies section on numbers alone.
- **068** is worth including purely for palette relief — everything else in Zombies is
  brown and firelit; the Thulian Archives are blue and flooded.
- **101** is the only shot with other real human players visible.
