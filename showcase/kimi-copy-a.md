## 3. Never an empty lobby

**Eyebrow: POPULATION**

The server browser will tell you 0/18. Every server, every time. That's not the whole story — it's just where the story starts.

Type `bot_fill 17` and every match fills on map start. Hardpoint, Kill Confirmed, TDM, Capture the Flag, Free-for-all, even War with full 9v9 teams. The moment a real player connects, a bot steps out and hands over its spot. The servers across the four hosts — CR's, Glooples, NamelessNoobs and the rest — are real, joinable, and waiting. They just don't sit around being busy.

The bots don't all sound the same, either. Three name pools, 54 names each, reshuffled every map. Default gives you Domino, Causeway, Overlord, Juno. Modern gives you BlindSprint, Reaver22, bleakwinter. And Nostalgia gives you the lobby you remember — xXDarkAngelXx, NoSc0pe360, CamperKing2010 — because an empty game should at least feel like 2017.

- **War, Operation Husky, 9v9.** Full teams on both sides of a War match — bot_fill doesn't skip the big modes.
- **Domination on Shipment, 97–114.** Mid-match, Modern pool in the feed — Reaver22 and friends holding their own.
- **TDM on Shipment, 26–25, 9:08 left.** A one-point game deep into the clock, Nostalgia pool — CamperKing2010 is exactly where you'd expect.

## 4. The match pays out

**Eyebrow: PROGRESSION**

You remember the end-of-match screen. Soldier levels up, fanfare plays, and the game tells you a Rare Supply Drop is yours. In retail, that was a promise with nothing behind it — the level-up registered, and the drop simply never arrived.

In S2x-CR it arrives. Every soldier level-up credits the Rare Supply Drop the screen always said you earned. No shop, no shortcut, no asterisk — the game makes a promise at the end of a match, and now there's something on the other end to honour it.

**Screenshots (s4):**

- **Top Players, end of match.** The board at full time, supply drops credited to the soldiers who earned them — the screen finally telling the truth.

## 5. Headquarters economy

**Eyebrow: THE GRIND, REBUILT**

Headquarters was the part of WWII that lived between matches — orders to chase, contracts to buy, payroll to collect, a Quartermaster to visit. When the servers went quiet, all of it went with them.

S2x-CR brings the whole loop back, re-implemented locally over the Achievement Engine protocol. Daily and weekly orders tick over on real timers. Contracts cost credits and pay out XP. Payroll shows up. The Quartermaster is open, and the Mail tab has things in it. None of it phones home — it runs here, because this is where the game lives now.

**Screenshots (s5):**

- **Rifle Adept, complete.** A daily order finished, next reset in 2h 54m — the timer is real and so is the next one.
- **Headshot Contract, 06h 40m.** 250 credits up front, 3,000 XP if you finish it in time. Your call.
- **The Quartermaster.** Drops on the shelf at 1,000 Armory Credits — duplicates you've converted finally spend somewhere.

## 6. Supply drops

**Eyebrow: THE FLIP**

The card flip is intact — the whole ceremony. Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary, three cards face down and a second of silence before you turn them. Duplicates don't sting anymore: they convert straight into Armory Credits, which feed the Quartermaster, which feeds the next drop.

You don't have to open them in a menu, either. Call a drop in on foot in Headquarters — walk to the marker, the crate comes down, and the cards come out of it. The way it was supposed to feel.

**Screenshots (s6):**

- **Legendary, mid-flip.** Gold on the table before the cards settle. Still the best sound in the game.
- **A duplicate, converting.** Already own it? Fine — it folds into Armory Credits on the spot.
- **Three Epics, one Rare drop.** Eastern Dragon charm, Task Force 9, Partigiani — a single Rare drop going purple end to end.
- **Crate down in the field.** Called in on foot in HQ — walk up, watch it land, open it where it fell.
