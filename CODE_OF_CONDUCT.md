# Code of conduct

This project and its Discord are a small community of people who want to play Call of Duty: WWII. The rules are the same in both places.

1. **Be respectful.** No slurs, hate speech, harassment, doxxing or personal attacks. Trash talk in a match is fine; being a person about it is not optional.
2. **No cheating.** No aimbots, wallhacks, exploits or modified files that give an unfair advantage, and no discussion of how to make them. This applies to contributions too.
3. **No piracy.** You need a legitimate Steam copy of the game. Do not share, request or discuss other ways to get it.
4. **English in public channels**, so everyone can follow the conversation.
5. **No spam** or self-promotion without asking first.

Breaking rules 1 to 3 is an immediate ban. Rules 4 and 5 get a warning, then a mute, then a ban. Maintainers have the final say on grey areas, and one appeal per ban is heard by direct message.

To report a problem, message **chrono** on the community Discord or use the contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
